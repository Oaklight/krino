  • KL 0.5: 0.4747 at seed 42 to 0.4538 / 0.4545 at seeds 43/44.

On 231 items, the smallest detectable difference at 80% power is 10.8 pp. On the hard 111 it is 15.7 pp. A true 2 pp effect has 4% power on the hard tier.

Inference: “not promoted” means underpowered at this effect size, not disproven.

Why listwise was not promoted

  • Pooled 4 wins, 0 losses, but the 4 wins are 2 distinct items. Pooling treats them as independent, so p = 0.125 overstates the evidence.
  • Every seed is below the +2.0 pp effect floor: +0.5 to +1.1 pp.

What would change the verdict

A true +2 pp effect needs 6,357 items at 80% power. Pairwise was the last set-level variant left to try, and it did not separate from control either.

Is the frozen backbone the ceiling?

Every trained head on a frozen 4B moved JevBench by 0.5–1.5 pp, pointwise and set-level alike. The one large move was the backbone: +7.3 pp with zero training. Should we stop head architecture and move to unfreezing, a tuned LoRA, a larger backbone, or distillation into the backbone?

Source. research/24-v3-closeout.md; research/23-public-tier-power.md; Trilium progress log 2026-11-02; results/gate/v4_listwise_*.
My read: the heads gain about 1.7 points in domain and 0.6 to 1.1 on transfer, and the full-layer LoRA sweep that gained 6 points in domain scores well below the frozen model on JevBench. Unfreezing is the largest untested lever, and I would pair it with data built for the abilities rather than run it alone.
