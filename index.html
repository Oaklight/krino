What we have established

  • On JevBench public, the per-option judgement readout beats option-text continuation on 13 of 14 checkpoints; on the 9 checkpoints where it wins by more than 10 pp the gap is 10.4–18.6 pp, largest on the original tier. openbmb/MiniCPM3-4B (instruct) is the exception (-12.1 pp).
  • Post-training that moves the objective toward the readout helps: a 4B base scores 53.7%, while the three-checkpoint 4B judgement band (Qwen/Qwen3-4B, Qwen/Qwen3-Reranker-4B, microsoft/Phi-4-mini-instruct) scores 61.9–64.9%.
  • JevBench 231 cannot resolve differences below about 9.3 pp, which is why its top four checkpoints (Qwen/Qwen3-Reranker-4B, microsoft/Phi-4-mini-instruct, Qwen/Qwen3-Reranker-8B, zeroentropy/zerank-2-reranker) are mutually indistinguishable within 2.6 pp. The 812-row development set resolves 5–6 pp and separates Qwen/Qwen3-Reranker-4B vs Qwen/Qwen3-Reranker-8B (6.03 pp, p = 0.0005) where JevBench has them 1 item apart.

In-domain single-task result

Banking77, trained heads: 95.2% (Ettin-150m r128). Jev's reference is 75.0%. This is an in-domain single-task result; Jev's number is zero-shot across 77 classes, so the comparison is not like-for-like.
